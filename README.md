## 🎮 About the Project

**PEN FIGHT SIMULATOR** recreates the nostalgic classroom game of pen fighting as a digital browser experience.

Control your pen, aim your shots, build momentum, and knock your opponent out of the arena. Test your precision, timing, and strategy in an entertaining battle.

The project is designed to run directly in a modern web browser without requiring a complex installation process.

## ✨ Features

- 🖊️ Interactive pen fighting gameplay.
- 🎯 Drag-and-release aiming mechanics.
- 💥 Physics-inspired movement and collisions.
- ⚡ Boost mechanics for stronger attacks.
- 🤖 AI opponent gameplay.
- 👥 Local two-player gameplay on the same device.
- 🏆 Round-based scoring and victory conditions.
- 🔊 Sound controls.
- ⏸️ Pause functionality.
- 🎨 Interactive visual interface and animations.
- 🌐 Browser-based gameplay.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Select your preferred game mode or difficulty.
3. Control the active pen using your mouse or supported touch controls.
4. Pull the pen backwards to aim your shot.
5. Release to launch the pen.
6. Use boosts strategically to improve your attacks.
7. Knock your opponent out of the playing area.
8. Win three rounds to claim victory!
